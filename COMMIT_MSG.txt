Log report Batch 3: activity timeline with brush-select and click-to-zoom

GET /api/logs/timeseries plus a chart above the grid, sharing the filter bar and the source selector.

Bucketing happens in Java rather than in SQL. Counting per time bucket needs a dialect-specific date
function, and H2 cannot be exercised in the chat sandbox, so the query returns a bounded projection of
timestamp and severity and the grouping is done in code: no dialect dependency and the sizing logic is
testable. The projection is capped at four hundred thousand rows and the response carries a truncated
flag so the page can say so instead of silently lying.

The auto bucket picks the smallest step that keeps the chart under about a hundred and sixty bars,
while minute, hour, day and week force a width. Measured: one hour gives one-minute buckets and sixty
bars, a day gives fifteen minutes and ninety-six bars, thirty days gives six hours and a hundred and
twenty bars, ninety days gives one day and ninety bars, a year gives one week and fifty-two bars. Run
statuses are mapped onto the same severity palette, so the chart reads the same in both sources.

The chart is stacked bars per bucket coloured by severity, with failures at the bottom so they are
always visible, native SVG tooltips, and no library or CDN — the same SVG-only approach already used
for the workflow diagram. Clicking a bar zooms into that bucket, dragging selects a range, and both
write into the from and to filters and re-run the search, so the chart, the grid and the filters remain
a single state; Full range clears them. A drag shorter than four units counts as a click, so the two
gestures never fight each other.
