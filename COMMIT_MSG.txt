feed schema index batch 2: the tree viewer over the index

feed_index_viewer.html at the repository root, beside csv-viewer.html:
a standalone SOURCE -> feed -> fields tree over the CSV that
Get-FeedSchemaIndex.ps1 writes, with one search box over sources,
feeds, fields, descriptions and variable values. 94 jsdom assertions on
the page plus 9 driving the real page with the real tool's output, and
14 mutations all caught. Nothing under src/ is touched.

Zero external references, asserted by scan rather than intended, and
the CSV splitter is csv-viewer.html's lifted verbatim: two
implementations of RFC-4180 quoting that quietly disagree are worse
than one. No literal escape sequences in the JavaScript, with a
positive control proving that scan can fire, plus the Thymeleaf-hostile
sequences and the duplicate top-level declaration scan that two earlier
single-file pages needed.

Columns are found by name and never by position. The index gains four
columns under -Detailed and one per -Variables entry, so a positional
reader would shift silently while looking correct on the default file.
That is precisely why the first mutation of this rule came back green,
and it stayed green until a -Detailed fixture was added.

Tables are drawn on demand and search runs on the model, so a match
inside a feed that has never been rendered is still found. A hit must
never look empty: a matching source shows all of its feeds and a
matching feed all of its fields, and counts read "1 of 3 fields" so a
filtered node cannot be mistaken for a small one.

The three findings the tool reports are drawn and not only counted:
orphan rows are marked and named, mandatory fields say YES, and a
nullable disagreement carries a badge whose tooltip names both values.
The end-to-end check asserts the page marks exactly the three
disagreements the tool counted on the same file. An unresolved
${runDate} token is coloured apart from an empty cell, so the
distinction the tool goes out of its way to preserve survives into the
picture instead of dying at the last step.

Three mutations came back green and all three were opened. One was a
bad mutation: removing the BOM strip from parseCsv changes nothing
because buildModel indexes on columns[i].trim() and JavaScript's trim
removes U+FEFF as whitespace, so the protection exists twice. The other
two were real holes - no fixture exercised -Detailed, and the
step-navigation test searched and stepped without collapsing anything
in between, so applyQuery had already performed step's work.

NOT VERIFIED: the real index, since the end-to-end run is this
repository's samples through the real tool and the real page rather
than 144 feeds; and the UBS browser, where flex-with-wrap throughout
and no grid layout is an argument and not a rendering. mvn clean
package is unaffected: the page is not in the WAR.
