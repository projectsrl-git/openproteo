designer: batchBy must redraw too, and an advisory scan for the class

After the content-source defect, the other conditional panels were audited
with a scan rather than by eye. Thirteen controls came back flagged and twelve
of them were fine.

Text inputs bound with oninput cannot redraw at all: doing it on every
keystroke takes the focus away mid-word, so there the redraw would be the
defect rather than the fix. Most of the rest branch only to set a checked or
selected attribute, which is already correct at the moment the value is
written.

ONE REAL CASE, MILDER THAN THE FIRST

batchBy hides nothing - maxBytesPerBatch and oversizeDocumentPolicy stay
visible on purpose, labelled NOT in effect - but it decides that labelling, so
switching to BYTES left three fields wrongly labelled until something else
redrew the panel. Same class as the content-source defect: a control that
changes what the panel says must not leave the old wording under the new
choice. Now elarSetBatchBy.

THE SCAN, AND WHY IT IS NOT A GATE

tools/scan_panel_redraw.js reports controls whose value decides the shape of
the panel and whose handler only stores it. It is advisory and exits 0 by
design. The first version flagged thirteen of which twelve were fine, and a
check that cries wolf twelve times out of thirteen is switched off within a
week, at which point it protects nothing. It stays diagnostic until it has
been calibrated against panels written after it.

Two things about the scan itself are worth keeping. Its own first run was a
reassuring lie: parameter names sit inside escaped quotes in the emitted
strings, so matching plain quotes found 3 parameters instead of 118 and
reported a clean bill of health. A scan that finds nothing has to be checked
for whether it can find anything at all. And it was proved to bite by
re-introducing each defect separately, helper deleted and handler reverted,
then confirming it reports that one and only that one.

VERIFIED
- The scan goes from 13 flagged to 0 with the two real defects fixed, and
  reports contentSource alone or batchBy alone when either is re-introduced.
- 116 designer assertions, up from 112: the batch-by select is reachable, its
  handler is not a bare setNodeParam, the NOT-in-effect wording is present
  under DOCUMENTS and gone after switching to BYTES, and the byte fields are
  still there because they were never hidden. Against the reverted template
  that suite fails 2 assertions.
- The elarcheck panel suite and the template scans unchanged.

NOT VERIFIED
- mvn clean package: Maven Central is unreachable from the chat sandbox, so
  the build on the target machine remains the only final proof. A Thymeleaf
  template and a standalone diagnostic script changed here; the template
  cannot be rendered without the internal Nexus and was checked structurally
  and under jsdom.
- The scan's classifier has been calibrated against the panels that exist
  today and against two re-introduced defects. It has never seen a panel
  written after it, which is the whole reason it is advisory.
