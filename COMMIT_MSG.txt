orchestrator.bash-exe (default /bin/bash), never as a file. Parameters

"bash -c" bootstrap ending in exec - a JVM without a UTF-8 locale turns
a non-ASCII argument or environment value into "?". Refused before
launch, reason in the step log: name collision, nameless parameter,
over 120 KiB, NUL, CRLF line endings.

started under setsid and, on timeout or Stop, killed by session and
descent after being stopped. Degraded modes are logged and shown on
the Platform page. destroyForcibly() alone left every child alive.

per run and concurrent items overwrote it. Changes behaviour on every
platform.
