objunpack: pre-check template sends a valid package without unpacking

Packages of 100 000 objects mostly need no correction; unpacking and
repacking each one costs minutes and twice the disk.

- scripts/objunpack-precheck.sh (bash; Linux and AIX): verifies the
  md5, extracts only the audit and the metadata CSV, publishes the
  record count the audit declares and whether the counts agree.
- validate: onFailedChecks = fail (default) | continue. With continue
  the step succeeds and ${checksFailed} carries the result, for a gate
  to branch on. Absent or fail: exactly as before.
- workflows/_TEMPLATE-objunpack-precheck-resend.xml: script, validate,
  gate; a coherent, verified, valid package goes straight to the FTPS
  step as it arrived, anything else through the existing corrective
  chain. _TEMPLATE-objunpack-resend.xml is unchanged.
- Designer: "When a check fails" on the validate panel; the option in
  the variables page.

The short cut does not run objunpack's per-object checks: it trusts
the checksum, three counts and the metadata checks. USAGE.md says so.

The script has no Windows version. USAGE.md and the objunpack spec
(section 20) updated. README.md and CLAUDE.md not touched.

Verified on Linux (sandbox; Temurin 1.8.0_432 and JDK 21.0.12; bash
5.2.21, GNU tar 1.35; LANG=C.UTF-8): the script against objunpack's
own reading of 15 packages, refusals and a 100 000-object package,
280 assertions as root, 275 as uid 65534; validate's real body before
and after, 375 assertions, identical without the parameter; the
template parsed and walked with the real resolver, gate evaluation,
send plan and bash runner, 12 scenarios; the panel in jsdom; 61
mutations, 4 equivalent and explained in the spec.

Verified on Windows: nothing.

Not verified on either: anything on AIX (its tar, csum, bash); the
engine running the template; a real FTPS send; Jackson reading the
dataschema (a stub stood in for it); a browser; mvn clean package.
