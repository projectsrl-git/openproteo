elarxml IFS: STAT becomes the lookup default, measured against a real store

The first real feed inverted a design decision. The documents live in
/Proteo/DOC/PDF and /Proteo/DOC/TIFF, which hold three million files between
them, and a run references a few thousand of them.

WHY THE LISTING DEFAULT WAS WRONG

The specification reasoned from the wrong axis. It asked how many directories
the paths were spread over and concluded that few directories meant few round
trips. True, and irrelevant: the cost of a listing is not the round trip, it
is everything in the directory. Three million entries would have been
transferred to reach a few thousand and held at roughly 300 MB to do it, and
the entry cap of 500000 would have fired on the first real run. The failure
would at least have carried a clear message, but the design would still have
been wrong.

The axis that matters is the ratio, not the count: how much of a directory a
feed actually uses. A stat per document is bounded by the feed; a listing is
bounded by the store behind it.

contentIfsLookup=STAT, the new default, asks per document: one round trip and
one cached entry for each document the feed references. It is right whenever a
feed touches a small fraction of a large store, which is what a document
archive is. LISTING remains for the opposite shape, a small directory that a
feed uses densely, where one round trip replaces thousands.

Changing this default is not an exception to the conservative-default rule,
and the distinction is worth keeping: IFS reading has never run in the field,
so no delivered feed changes behaviour. A default that fails on the only real
feed we have is simply the wrong way round.

The cap message now names the strategy, because the same number means two
different things. Under LISTING it counts every file in the store and points
at contentIfsLookup=STAT. Under STAT it counts the documents the feed
references, so tripping it means the feed itself is larger than expected.

VERIFIED
- 162 assertions on the store, up from 144. The decisive one reproduces the
  reported shape - two directories of three thousand documents, six of them
  referenced - and asserts 0 listings and 6 stats under the default, against 1
  listing pulling 3000 entries to reach one under LISTING.
- Both cap messages, each asserted to explain its own situation rather than
  just refusing.
- End to end, the two strategies deliver a byte-identical INDX: 30 documents
  with 0 listings and 30 stats one way, 3 listings and 0 stats the other, and
  30 network transits either way, so the staging still works under both.
- 118 designer assertions, up from 116, covering the new field and that STAT
  is the one selected by default. All seven elar suites, the elarcheck panel,
  the template scans and the panel-redraw scan unchanged.

NOT VERIFIED
- mvn clean package: Maven Central is unreachable from the chat sandbox, so
  the build on the target machine remains the only final proof.
  IfsContentStore was compiled and executed against the elar package; the
  lines added to runElarXml cannot be compiled here, since the engine package
  needs Spring from the internal Nexus.
- Jt400Ifs has still never been executed. Nothing in this commit touches it:
  the strategy is decided above it, and both branches call methods it already
  exposed.
