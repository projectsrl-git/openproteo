unarchive: batch C, opt-in deletion after extraction; batch R specified

Gate 0 of 2026-10-04: flat layout stays excluded; FIFOs and devices
unchanged; zip, tar and gz suffice; non-UTF-8 tar names stay refused;
deletion after extraction and nested archives become opt-in features.

afterExtract=delete: the archive is deleted only after its own commit -
never when its extraction failed or was stopped, never when it was
skipped. A failed deletion fails the step and says the extraction was
committed. Designer option with a no-undo warning, validation,
PARAM_OPTIONS, USAGE.md.

The spec (section 22) also specifies batch R, nested archives: opt-in,
selected by name only (Office files are zips), depth 3 by default,
extracted in place, removed after their own extraction, limits counted
over the whole tree, all or nothing per outer archive.
