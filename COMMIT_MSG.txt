Log report Batch 4: metric cards that double as filters

GET /api/logs/metrics computes the stat numbers over exactly the same filtered set as the grid and the
chart, and a card row above the chart displays them.

For the events source: events, runs touched, feeds touched, failures, successes, the average step
duration and the top five feeds and steps by failure. For the runs source: runs, feeds touched,
succeeded, failed together with the total of failed steps, the average run duration and the top five
feeds by failure.

Counting is done in SQL. Durations are paired in Java -- STEP_STARTED to STEP_COMPLETED or STEP_FAILED
for the same feed, run and node, read in an order that makes the pairing deterministic -- because date
arithmetic is dialect specific and H2 cannot be exercised in the chat sandbox; the projection is capped
at three hundred thousand rows. Runs use end minus start. An unpaired start, from a run still in flight
or a step that never completed, contributes nothing rather than skewing the average, and when there is
no pair at all the card shows a dash instead of a misleading zero.

The cards are also a filter surface: failures and successes select that severity, a feed in the top
feeds list selects that feed, and a step in the top steps list sets the step filter together with
severity FAIL. Every click goes through the same search used by the filter bar, so the cards, the chart
and the grid remain a single state and a card click also redraws the chart and recomputes the cards. The
step filter is part of the query state and is cleared by Reset.

Docs: .claude/2026-08-03-log-report-batch4.md and CLAUDE.md.

Verify: every metric statement -- the five counters and both top-N queries -- and the duration pairing
were executed against a real SQL engine on a fixture with two feeds, two runs and three step pairs: the
counters came back 6/2/2/2/1, the top step was DEQUOTE with two failures, and the average step duration
23.3 seconds over the expected twenty, ten and forty second pairs. logs.html passes node --check with
zero literal \n or \r and no unsafe Thymeleaf, and brace balance was checked with the string and comment
aware counter.
