Copy file list: USAGE.md, and the feature is complete (batch 4)

The section «ifscopy: copying the files listed in a CSV» becomes «Copying
the files listed in a CSV (ifscopy, filecopy, safecopy)» and covers all
three. One section, not three: three would drift, which is the argument
that made the reader shared in batch 1 and the panel builder shared in
batch 3, and most of what there is to say is identical, so three copies
would mostly be three chances to contradict each other. The filecopy and
safecopy entries in the executor list said nothing about the option at all,
and ifscopy's cross-reference pointed at the old title.

What differs between the three is written AS a difference, with its reason:
what counts as a name that already says where the file is on each side, and
why a leading slash is in the local list - on Windows it means the root of
the CURRENT drive, which is exactly the shape a list from an earlier
ifscopy step carries; the backslash left alone in both, for opposite
reasons; the pre-scan filecopy and safecopy have and ifscopy deliberately
does not, so the default policy copies nothing at all rather than stopping
half way; safecopy's refusal of a listed name already ending in the temp
suffix; filecopy being copy-only with a list; the one flat destination; and
the output names kept as they were rather than renamed for symmetry, which
would break every existing gate.
